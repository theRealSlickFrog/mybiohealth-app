# 1. Core Framework

*My*BioHealth™ organizes health information through the following framework:

Raw Measures → *My*BioSignals™ → *My*Trajectory™ → *My*Strategy™ → *My*Consult™

The value of the Platform arises not from individual laboratory results, but from the organization of health information into signals, patterns, trajectories, and actions over time. *My*BioHealth’s unique value lies in helping Members understand where they are, where they are heading, and which actions may influence future outcomes.

# 2. Definitions

“Member Data” means personal information, health information, laboratory results, uploaded records, device or measurement data, responses, and other information that you or someone on your behalf supplies to the Platform, including recordings and transcripts of your sessions with us.

“Derived Content” means notes, summaries, strategies, reports, scores, visualizations, and consultation records generated on the Platform from Member Data. See section 7.

“De-identified Data” means information from which direct identifiers have been removed and which, in our reasonable assessment, does not identify an individual and cannot reasonably be re-identified.

“*My*BioSignals™” means *My*BioHealth’s framework for interpreting health information as meaningful health signals.

“*My*Trajectory™” means *My*BioHealth’s longitudinal framework for interpreting direction, velocity, burden, confidence, trajectory bend, and related measures over time. It may include Trajectory Direction™, Trajectory Velocity™, Trajectory Burden™, Trajectory Confidence™, Trajectory Bend™, *My*Trajectory Index™, and future methodologies.

“*My*Strategy™” means educational suggestions, discussion points, habits, interventions, and action plans.

“*My*Consult™” means structured consultations, discussions, reviews, summaries, reports, and related workflows.

“Kenesis™” means educational content, courses, media, curated resources, podcasts, lectures, discussion forums, and related learning experiences.
